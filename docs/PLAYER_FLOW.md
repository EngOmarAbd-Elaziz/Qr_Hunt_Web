# Player Flow

1. Player scans the Master QR Code at the entrance.
2. They are prompted for their Name. A `HUNT-XXXXX` Player Code is generated.
3. They enter the Game Dashboard.
4. They run around scanning QR Codes using their native camera or the app scanner.
5. Fragments show up as letters in "Your Collection".
6. Players drag and drop letters into the "Hidden Words" slots.
7. They press "Check Word".
8. If correct, they see the Winner Screen permanently.
